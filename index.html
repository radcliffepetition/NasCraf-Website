FAQ
How are ranks decided?

Each gamemode has a hidden ELO. Your first 3 matches are placements that start you anywhere from Coal I to Iron III. After that every round you win or lose moves your ELO, and Season Points (SP) from all modes decide your overall rank.

What is Champion?

One player per gamemode: the #1 player on that mode's leaderboard with at least the Champion ELO.

Can I lose ELO by winning?

No. Winning a match never loses ELO and losing never gains it.

What are coins for?

You earn coins from every ranked match and spend them in the in-game Item Shop on emotes and death animations.

What are parties?

Groups of up to 8 friends. The host picks two members to fight unranked duels in the arena, and everyone else can spectate.

RULES
Play fair

No hacked clients, macros or exploits. Matches are recorded and suspicious results can be removed.

Don't leave matches

Leaving a started match counts as a forfeit and loses the remaining rounds.

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech or spam in messages or chat. You can set messages to friends-only at any time.

One account each

Don't boost your rank with alt accounts or arranged matches.
